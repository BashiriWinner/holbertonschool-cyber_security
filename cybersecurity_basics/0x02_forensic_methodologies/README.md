# Forensic Ethics & Methodologies



## 📌 Project Overview

This project explores the fundamental intersection of **Digital Forensics**, **Legal Standards**, and **Professional Ethics**. In a field where data integrity can determine the outcome of legal proceedings, understanding the methodology and moral responsibilities of an investigator is paramount.

---

## 🎓 Learning Objectives

By the completion of this project, I have gained a deep understanding of:

* **The Ethical Core:** Why integrity and objectivity are the pillars of forensic analysis.
* **Methodological Standards:** Mastering the ACPO principles and NIST frameworks.
* **Chain of Custody:** The technical and legal requirements for evidence admissibility.
* **The Forensic Process:** From initial acquisition to final courtroom documentation.

---

## ⚖️ Core Ethical Pillars

Maintaining ethical standards is not optional; it is the foundation of digital forensics.

* **Objectivity:** Evaluating evidence without bias, ensuring facts speak for themselves.
* **Integrity:** Ensuring that digital evidence remains unaltered from the moment of seizure.
* **Confidentiality:** Protecting sensitive data encountered during an investigation.

---

## 🛠 Digital Forensic Methodologies

This repository documents the application of standard forensic stages:

| Stage | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Identification** | Recognizing potential sources of evidence and scope of the incident. |
| **Preservation** | Isolating, securing, and documenting the state of digital evidence. |
| **Analysis** | Using specialized tools to extract and interpret data without modification. |
| **Documentation** | Maintaining a rigorous Chain of Custody and detailed investigative notes. |
| **Reporting** | Presenting findings in a clear, objective, and legally admissible format. |

---

## 📜 Standards & Compliance

This project references and adheres to guidelines provided by international forensic authorities:

* **ACPO:** Association of Chief Police Officers (UK) - The 4 Principles of Computer Forensics.
* **NIST:** National Institute of Standards and Technology.
* **SWGDE:** Scientific Working Group on Digital Evidence.
* **DFRWS:** Digital Forensic Research Workshop.
